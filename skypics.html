What is Skypics?

Skypics is my flagship project — a social media platform unlike anything that exists today. It's built specifically for people who love capturing the beauty of the sky, nature, clouds, sunsets, and everything in between.

Unlike Instagram or other platforms, Skypics is 100% focused on nature photography. No selfies, no food, no ads — just pure, breathtaking sky and nature content.

The platform uses AI to moderate, enhance, and categorize every photo uploaded. Bad photos get filtered out automatically. Great photos get showcased in a stunning 3D immersive environment.

Think of it as Instagram meets the Metaverse meets AI — all built around the beauty of the natural world.
